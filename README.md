# Tiny Guy

**A tiny pixel companion that lives inside your browser! Earn XP, level up, and watch your little buddy come alive on every webpage.**

Tiny Guy drops a cute, retro pixel-art character directly onto whatever website you're visiting. He follows your cursor, leaves a colorful trail of pixel dust, earns XP as you browse, and celebrates every level-up with a burst of confetti!

---

## Game Features

- **Cursor Companion**: He faithfully follows your cursor across every single page.
- **Leveling System**: Every pixel you travel gives you XP. Grind those levels and unlock celebrations!
- **Step Counter**: Tracks your total distance traveled across the entire internet.
- **Pixel Dust Trail**: Awesome colorful square particles trail behind him in true retro fashion.
- **Idle Animation**: Leave him alone for 5 seconds and he'll do a cute little idle bounce.
- **Gameboy Dashboard**: A beautifully styled retro Gameboy popup menu to manage your companion.
- **WASD Keyboard Mode**: Tired of the mouse? Flip the switch and drive him around the screen using W, A, S, D and left-click elements by pressing K!

---

## How to Play (Installation)

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions/
3. Flip the **Developer mode** switch on (top right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the TinyGuy project folder.
5. Click the extension icon to boot up the retro Gameboy control panel and start playing!

---

## Game Settings

Open the Tiny Guy Gameboy popup to configure your run:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|------------|
| **Power** | Turn the game engine on or off instantly. |
| **Scale** | Make him huge or shrink him down to microscopic size (1% to 25%). |
| **Speed** | Adjust his movement speed stat. |
| **Anim Speed** | Control the sprite animation framerate. |
| **Drop Shadow** | Toggle the retro shadow effect for extra depth. |
| **Keyboard Control**| Turn off mouse tracking to take manual control with WASD. |

## The Tech Engine

- Built with pure vanilla JavaScript (No heavy frameworks here!)
- Powered by Chrome Extension Manifest V3
- HTML5 Canvas for silky smooth 60fps rendering
- CSS3 with a fully custom retro Gameboy aesthetic
- Styled with Google Fonts (Orbitron)
